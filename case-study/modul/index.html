Case Study

MODUL
Kitchens engineered like precision products

Timeline: 10 days
Pages: 6
Stack: HTML, CSS, JS, Canvas, GSAP
Role: Design & Development

Problem

MODUL sells precision-engineered modular kitchens — but their old site looked like a generic catalogue. No way to visualize a kitchen, no price transparency, and the configurator was a PDF form. They needed a site that proved their engineering chops: interactive, transparent, and technically impressive.

Approach

Cinematic Hero

Parallax background with particle canvas overlay. "Engineered kitchens. Crafted spaces." — gold gradient text, scroll-reveal stats. Sets the premium tone instantly.

Before/After Slider

Drag-to-compare 1990s kitchen → MODUL walk-in pantry. Canvas-based, touch-friendly, 60fps. The single most shared element on social.

Filterable Catalogue

Base/Wall/Tall/Corner/Island units with quick-view modals. Each card has specs, price, and "Add to quote" — all data-driven from a JSON file.

Design Studio (Quote Builder)

Real-time configurator: layout → finish → countertop → appliances. Running estimate updates instantly. Saves to localStorage, emails formatted brief.
