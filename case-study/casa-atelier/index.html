Casa Atelier
Interiors that breathe

Build time: 7 days
Pages: 5
Stack: HTML, CSS, JS, Custom Design System
Role: Design & Development
Type: Self-initiated concept · fictional brand

The concept brief

This is a self-initiated concept for a fictional residential interior studio. The brief: replace a static PDF portfolio with a calm, editorial site that filters projects, explains pricing openly and books consultations without email ping-pong.

Approach

Typewriter Hero

Rotating headline — "love", "live in", "never want to leave" — typed at 110ms/char. Sets emotional tone before the first scroll.

Animated Counters

Four metrics (320+ homes, 14 years, 98% happy clients, 24 awards) count up on scroll via IntersectionObserver. No library, 2KB JS.

Filterable Gallery

Vanilla-JS isotope-style filter (Living/Bedroom/Kitchen) with lightbox. Zero dependencies, works offline, 60fps on mobile.

Transparent Pricing

Three packages with clear scope, timeline, and EMI note. No "contact for pricing" — builds trust before the first call.
