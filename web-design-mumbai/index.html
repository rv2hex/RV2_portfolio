Questions people ask

How much does a website cost in Mumbai?

Starter is ₹10,000 for a 3-page site, Standard is ₹20,000 for 5 pages, and Premium is ₹40,000 for a larger multi-page build. Every price is fixed before work starts — 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. There is no monthly fee and no hidden charge.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most small-business sites take 5–10 days from the day you send your content. A Standard 5-page build usually lands in about seven days.

Do you use templates or WordPress?

No. Every site is hand-coded in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no page builder, no theme licence and no plugin stack to break — which is why the sites stay fast and cheap to maintain.

Will my website work on a phone?

Yes. Every build is designed mobile-first, because most of your customers will find you on a phone. Speed and tap targets are checked before launch.

Can customers message me on WhatsApp from the site?

Yes. A WhatsApp button with a pre-written message is included as standard, so a visitor can start a conversation in one tap.

Which areas of Mumbai do you work with?

Mumbai, Thane and Navi Mumbai — including Andheri, Bandra, Dadar, Powai, Malad, Borivali, Colaba, Chembur and Vashi. Work is handled remotely, so location is not a barrier.
