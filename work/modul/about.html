Our Story

From a two-man workshop to 2,400 kitchens

MODUL started in 2013 as a two-man carpentry workshop in Peenya. Our first kitchen took eleven days. Today our plant produces 40 kitchens a month, yet every one is still built on the same three rules — marine-grade ply, European hardware, and a price we'd be comfortable paying ourselves. We measure every space, render it in 3D, and never start cutting until you've approved it on paper.
