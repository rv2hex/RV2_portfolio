The Studio

A design-led studio for life at home

Founded in 2012 in Mumbai, Casa Atelier began as a two-person studio with a single belief — a home should tell your story. Today we lead a senior team of interior designers, architects and craftsmen who turn every brief into a living, breathing space.

  • End-to-end turnkey delivery — design to decor to move-in
  • Ethically sourced teak, sheesham & local Indian craft
  • Dedicated project manager for every home
  • Transparent, milestone-based pricing with no hidden costs
