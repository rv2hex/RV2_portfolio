Our Story

Every home should tell your story

Casa Atelier began in 2012 as a two-person studio in Mumbai with a single belief — a home should be more than an interior; it should be a reflection of the people who live in it.

Today we are a senior team of interior designers, architects and craftsmen who have delivered over 320 homes. We work with ethically sourced teak, sheesham and mango wood, blending modern design with the warmth of Indian craft.

  • End-to-end turnkey delivery — design to decor to move-in
  • Ethically sourced teak, sheesham & local Indian craft
  • Dedicated project manager for every home
  • Transparent, milestone-based pricing with no hidden costs
