About Us

Crafting comfort for Indian homes since 2010

Our Story

Founded in 2010 in Mumbai, Furniture Now! started as a small family workshop with a passion for quality craftsmanship.

Over the years, we have grown into one of India's most trusted furniture brands, serving over 15,000 happy customers across the country. Our commitment to using sustainable Sheesham and Teak wood with innovative designs sets us apart.

We believe every Indian home deserves beautifully crafted furniture without burning a hole in your pocket. That's why we offer premium quality at honest prices.
