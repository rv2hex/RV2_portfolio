Our story · Est. 2009

Built slowly.
Lived in daily.

We are a Mumbai furniture studio with a workshop, a showroom and a stubborn belief that the things you live with should get better with time.

Kandivali West, Mumbai 16 years of making 15,000+ homes
01 / The beginning

A small workshop.
A long view.

Furniture Now! began in 2009 with two carpenters, a rented workshop in Kandivali and a first order for a teak dining table. We did not set out to build a brand. We set out to make things properly.

That first table is still in use. So is the idea behind it: choose honest materials, detail the joints, finish the surfaces by hand and let the piece earn its place in a home.

Today we work with interior designers, architects and homeowners across Mumbai and beyond. The scale has changed. The standard has not.

02 / What we believe

Four things we
won't compromise.

A piece of furniture is a small daily relationship. These are the rules we use to make sure it starts and stays good.

01

Material honesty

Real timber, real stone, real linen. We choose materials that age visibly and gracefully, not surfaces that pretend.

02

Made to be lived in

Comfort is not a compromise. We prototype, sit, test and adjust until a piece works for real rooms and real people.

03

Repair over replace

We keep frames, hardware and fabric details serviceable. A mark can be restored; a good piece should not be disposable.

04

Fair craft

Our makers are paid for the skill they bring. Good work takes time, and the price reflects the hands behind it.

03 / How it happens

From a room
to a finished piece.

01

Visit

Come to the showroom or send us plans. We look at the space, the light and how you actually use it.

02

Plan

We agree the proportions, materials and finish, then confirm the lead time and the final quote.

03

Make

The frame is built, the upholstery is cut and the piece is finished and checked in our Kandivali workshop.

04

Place

We deliver, assemble and position the piece, take away the packaging and make sure the room feels right.

04 / The workshop

The part
you don't see.

Behind every finished piece is a workshop that smells of teak, a wall of samples and a team that knows exactly how much sanding a curve needs.

We keep production small on purpose. It lets us check every joint, match every grain and make the small adjustments that separate a piece of furniture from a product.

  • Kiln-dried hardwood frames
  • Hand-cut joinery
  • Water-based finishes
  • Repairable hardware
  • Small-batch upholstery
  • 10-year frame warranty
