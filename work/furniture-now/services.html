Services · End to end

From first sketch
to final cushion.

Whether you need one sofa or a whole room, we can help with the layout, the making, the delivery and the last small decisions that make a space feel finished.

Consultation from ₹5,000 Adjusted against your order Mumbai & beyond
01 / What we do

Six ways we
make it work.

Start with a single piece or hand us the whole room. The service scales to the project, not the other way around.

01

Interior consultation

A focused session on layout, scale, colour and the pieces that will do the most work in your room. Bring plans, photos or just the measurements.

02

Space planning

A measured plan with furniture placement, circulation and storage. Useful before you commit to a sofa, a table or a full renovation.

03

Made to measure

Change the dimensions, the fabric or the finish of any piece in the collection. We prototype and confirm before production begins.

04

Upholstery & restoration

A good frame deserves a second life. We reupholster, repair joinery and refresh finishes on pieces that still have years in them.

05

Delivery & assembly

Careful delivery, on-site assembly and packaging removal. We place the piece, check the finish and leave the room ready to use.

06

Trade program

For interior designers, architects and studios: trade pricing, finish samples, lead-time support and one contact for the whole project.

02 / What it includes

A real person,

03 / The process

Slow where it
matters.

01

Listen

We ask how the room is used, what is staying and what has never worked. No mood board before the brief.

02

Draw

You see the proportions, materials and layout before anything is cut. Changes are cheaper on paper.

03

Make

One workshop, one team. We update you at the frame, upholstery and finishing stages.

04

Place

We deliver, assemble and style the piece. If something is not right, we fix it before we leave.
