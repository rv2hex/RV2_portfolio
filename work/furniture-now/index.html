02 / Our point of view

Good furniture is a quiet luxury.

We started in 2009 with a small workshop, a stack of timber and a simple idea: make furniture that feels as good on the tenth year as it did on the first day.

Today, every piece is still drawn, prototyped and finished by people who care about the grain, the joint and the way a room feels when you walk into it.
